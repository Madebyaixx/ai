# CLAUDE / SHOWREEL 2026

A 15-second, 1080p60 motion graphics showreel. Every frame is generated by code:
no stock footage, no templates, no external assets. The soundtrack is original and
synthesized from scratch, with every hit scored to an on-screen event.

**Watch:** [`out/claude-showreel-2026.mp4`](out/claude-showreel-2026.mp4)

## Structure

128 BPM, 8 bars, one bar (1.875 s) per scene. Every cut lands on a downbeat and
every scene change uses a different transition.

| # | Time | Scene | Craft on show | Transition in |
|---|------|-------|---------------|---------------|
| 1 | 0.00 | Intro | dot → anticipation → line → split-reveal title, typewriter | — |
| 2 | 1.88 | Form | shape morphing, squash & stretch, overshoot, follow-through trails | iris |
| 3 | 3.75 | Kinetic type | four words, four techniques: drop, tracking, wave, slam + slice glitch | slanted slash |
| 4 | 5.63 | Particles | 4,200-particle 3D sphere explodes and reassembles into a word | glitch slices |
| 5 | 7.50 | Data viz | staggered bars with elastic data swap, donut, draw-on line chart | rising blinds |
| 6 | 9.38 | 3D / space | retro horizon, wireframe icosahedron, chromatic type, dolly-through | spinning diamond |
| 7 | 11.25 | Generative | 144 Truchet tiles rippling in waves from changing origins | white flash punch-in |
| 8 | 13.13 | End card | every shape converges, shockwave, logo mark builds, signature | tile collapse |

Global layer: camera shake impulses on hits, HUD with live timecode and beat pulse
(difference-blended so it reads on every background), film grain, vignette, and a
180° shutter motion blur rendered from 6 sub-frames.

## Files

- `index.html` – the engine. Open it in a browser for a real-time preview
  (space: play/pause, ←/→: scrub, 0: restart).
- `render.mjs` – frame-exact renderer: headless Chromium + ffmpeg, parallel workers.
- `audio.py` – soundtrack synth (numpy only).
- `build.sh` – full build: `./build.sh` → `out/claude-showreel-2026.mp4`.

Requires Node with Playwright (Chromium), Python 3 with numpy, and ffmpeg.
